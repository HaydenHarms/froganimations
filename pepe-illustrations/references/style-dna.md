# Style DNA

## In one line

Pure white, minimal, hand-drawn, lots of empty space, restrained, a bit absurd, product-sketch feel, handwritten notes, a clear structure that never turns into an instruction manual.

Like someone who has worked with AI, products, design and dev tools for years, sketching on white paper to explain one thing.

## Must

- 16:9 landscape (1600×900 SVG, rendered to 3200×1800 PNG).
- Pure white background: no beige, warm grey, paper texture, gradient, shadow, noise, or vintage paper.
- Black hand-drawn line art: fine lines, slight wobble (the `wobble` filter), not mechanical vector, no heavy outlines.
- Lots of white space: the main subject is about 40-60% of the canvas, at least 35% stays blank, ideally with one whole calm area.
- A few handwritten labels: 2-8 total, each 1-4 words (2-8 Chinese characters), in the source's language.
- One picture explains one core action, structure, state or metaphor.
- The structure speaks for itself. Never write its type name on the picture.
- Detail where things are drawn (see the detail floor in `svg-drawing-guide.md`). Minimal means few ideas and lots of space, not crude objects.

## Colour

- **Black** `#1a1a1a`: line art, objects, frames, structure, main labels.
- **Red** `#e0302a`: key notes, problems, emotional points, important warnings, results.
- **Orange** `#ef8a1f`: the main flow, paths, arrows, automation direction, movement from A to B.
- **Blue** `#2f6fd6`: side notes, inner state, system state, a second layer of explanation, AI/assistant/automation hints.
- **Pepe's greens and lip colour**: on Pepe only.

Blue isn't required in every picture. Use colour sparingly. Less is better.

## Never

- Commercial illustration.
- PPT infographic.
- Formal flowchart.
- Course slides.
- Cute cartoon poster.
- Children's illustration.
- Complex architecture diagram.
- Polished flat illustration.
- Techy UI.
- Real app screenshots.
- Complex backgrounds, gradients, shadows, textures.
- Explaining every node.
- A type title in the top-left ("Workflow", "System architecture", "Common pitfalls", "Roadmap").

## Taste

Strange, creative, interesting, clean, imaginative.

Not cute, not childish, not complicated, not stiff.
